Data provenance, methodology, and analytical framework · Barcelona, Catalonia
Analytical Framework

Providence integrates six independent open-source data streams into a unified operational picture of Barcelona. Each layer addresses a distinct analytical question and is sourced exclusively from official, publicly accessible APIs. No data is fabricated, interpolated, or estimated beyond the methods documented below. The platform is designed to demonstrate the core intelligence principle that geospatial correlation of independent datasets reveals patterns invisible in any single source.
